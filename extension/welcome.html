What it does

Claude's usage limit resets 5 hours after your first message. Pregame sends one tiny message for you as soon as a window resets, so when you start working your next reset is already on its way.

without: you start 9:00 ─────────────── reset 14:00 with: auto 6:00 ─── you start 9:00 ── reset 11:00
Two quick things
  1. Pin it: click the puzzle-piece icon in your toolbar, then the pin next to Pregame. The badge shows the time until your next reset.
  2. Pick your hours: open Pregame and set when it should work. Start it 2–4 hours before you usually begin.

Pings go into incognito chats, so they never show up in your history. Pregame runs while your browser is open.
